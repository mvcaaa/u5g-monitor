## What it shows (and does not)
- The slow state is again destination-specific: DNA ~1 Mbit/s while Cloudflare is fast, with clean ICMP, no loss and a stable radio.
- Heavy reordering (OFO) appears in the bad state for **both** destinations and disappears after the cure, but Cloudflare is fast even at 96 % OFO. So OFO is a marker of the bad state, **not** shown to be the cause of the slow DNA transfer.
- The cure worked **without a cell change** (cell A before and after). Cell A -> B was seen in earlier cures but is not required. What changed visibly: the LTE secondary carrier B1 became ACTIVE after the cure (it was absent in the 18:57 GSTATUS). Whether that is cause or consequence is unknown.
- Radio quality does not discriminate: LTE SINR was ~6.6 dB both during the episode and after the cure (the good-state baseline had 18.6 dB).
- Time to recover: the re-test ~4 min after starting the cure was good. The watchdog's re-measure at `WAITAFTER=170` s (~50 s after `00` is restored) was not exercised in live mode; see KNOWN_GAPS.
