## What it shows (and does not)
- DNA stays at ~1 Mbit/s for >30 minutes with clean ICMP, no packet loss counted by the receiver, an unchanged traceroute and a stable radio. Cloudflare was degraded and uneven during the episode, but never as low as DNA.
- Heavy TCP reordering (OFO) was present in the episode for both destinations (Cloudflare 96 % OFO at 42 Mbit/s), but OFO also reappeared **after** the cure (19:26-19:28, DNA at ~58 Mbit/s). It tracks the LTE carrier aggregation (B1 active) better than the slow-DNA state. **It is not a marker of the episode and not shown to cause it.**
- LTE B1 was active at times during the episode (e.g. 19:15-19:19) while DNA stayed at ~1 Mbit/s, so B1 being active or not does not separate good from bad either.
- The cure worked **without a cell change** (cell A before and after). Cell A -> B was seen in earlier cures but is not required.
- Radio quality does not discriminate: LTE SINR ~6.6 dB during the episode and after the cure (good-state baseline 18.6 dB).
- Recovery happened between 19:21:37 (0D set) and 19:25:07 (first good probe). The data cannot say whether it happened during the 0D hold or after `00` was restored; it was complete 90 s after `00`.
