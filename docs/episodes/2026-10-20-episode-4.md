## What it shows
- **The light-only detector works live:** detection 4 minutes after onset (3 samples in a row plus the probe/watchdog cron phase), no false clear
  during 1.5 h, clear 5 minutes after recovery.
- **No load before onset:** household WAN download 18:42-19:00 was 1-4 Mbit/s. The idea that long heavy downloads trigger episodes
  (episodes 1 and 2) is not supported here.
- **Recovery again during the `0D` hold**, i.e. as soon as the NR leg was released (see FINDINGS, M1).
- After the cure the radio changed more than in episodes 1 and 3: cell A -> cell B, NR ARFCN 152600 -> 156600, NR SNR ~20 dB -> ~2.5 dB,
  NR RSRP -68 -> -72 dBm. Speed was good regardless (light probe 0.5-0.8 s). This combination was seen in earlier cures (MODEM.md).
- The day limit (`MAXDAY` 4) was used up by detections of the former detector earlier that day, so no `diag` was saved for this episode.
  From 2026-10-21 only the light detector consumes triggers.

## Follow-up
- Episode tests (packet-size pings, MSS-limited downloads) now run automatically on each trigger (`agent/episode-tests.sh`); a healthy-state
  reference run at 20:41-20:43 gave 0 % loss at all sizes towards DNA and Cloudflare, RTT 36-44 ms, DNA 5 MB at 53-55 Mbit/s with the default MSS
  and 27-30 Mbit/s with MSS 1200/1000 (single run each, noisy).
