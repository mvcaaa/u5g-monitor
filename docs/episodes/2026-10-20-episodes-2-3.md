## What these show
- **Not evening peak load:** episode 2 ran through the night (lowest load), episode 3 at midday.
- **Episodes can end without a cure:** episode 2 lasted ~7.7 h and recovered by itself, with no band change and no modem restart.
- **Recovery happens during the 0D hold:** in episode 3 the light probe was fast 44-103 s after `0D` was set, while `00` was restored only at
  120 s. Episode 1 could not tell this apart. It supports testing a shorter hold (`band-cycle.sh 20`, open item).
- **Light probe detects earlier** than the heavy watchdog (12 and 9 minutes), but during episode 2 it cleared falsely five times while the heavy probe
  stayed slow. A fixed 2 s threshold is too close to the episode's 1 MB times; this is input for a baseline-relative light detector.
- Radio again does not discriminate: same cell and carriers as in the good state, SINR in the usual range.
